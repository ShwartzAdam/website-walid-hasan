The company

Building the infrastructure behind growing communities.

Walid Hasan is an infrastructure, development and earthworks contractor. We prepare the ground, lay the networks and build the roads that new neighborhoods and public projects depend on — with our own crews and our own heavy equipment, from the first cut to final handover.

How it's built

From ground to community.

  1. 01Ground

    Everything starts with the ground: survey, excavation, fill and compaction.

  2. 02Infrastructure

    Below the surface: water, sewer and drainage networks.

  3. 03Development

    Roads, sidewalks and retaining walls tie it all together.

  4. 04Community

    The place where people live.
